Add a company career board

Most companies post to their own board before anything reaches an aggregator. Each board is identified by a slug — the company's part of the board URL, e.g. gitlab in boards.greenhouse.io/gitlab. Slugs cannot be guessed, so add one and press Test: it reports how many postings came back and whether they include descriptions.
Where to find the slug
Greenhouse — boards.greenhouse.io/slug · Lever — jobs.lever.co/slug · SmartRecruiters — jobs.smartrecruiters.com/slug · Personio — slug.jobs.personio.de · Recruitee — slug.recruitee.com

Deleting a source that has stored postings is refused, because its jobs — and any applications tracked against them — would be deleted with it. Disable it instead.
